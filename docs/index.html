FAQ

Do I need Adobe Acrobat?

No. FillBatch works on its own.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your PC. Only your license key is checked once during activation.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The installer is new and not code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway. You can also use the portable ZIP.

My PDF isn't fillable.

Add form fields first with a free editor (LibreOffice Draw) or Acrobat's Prepare Form, then use FillBatch.

Which PDFs are supported?

Standard fillable PDFs (AcroForms). Dynamic XFA (LiveCycle) forms are not supported; the app tells you clearly if a form is XFA.

How many computers can I use?

Up to 3 computers that you own, with one license.

Mac or Linux?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only for now.
